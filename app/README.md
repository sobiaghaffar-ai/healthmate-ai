# 💙 HealthMate AI

## 📌 About The App

HealthMate AI is an AI-powered personal wellness assistant that helps users understand their daily health habits and receive personalized wellness suggestions.

It solves the problem of people struggling to maintain healthy routines like proper hydration, sleep, exercise, diet, and stress management.

The app is designed for students and individuals who want simple AI-based guidance for improving their daily lifestyle.

---

## 🌟 Features

- 📝 Collects daily health information from users
- 🤖 Generates AI-powered wellness reports
- 📊 Provides personalized wellness score
- 💡 Gives health improvement tips
- 🌱 Provides motivational messages
- 📋 Copy AI report feature
- 🔄 Reset form feature

---

## 🤖 AI Feature

HealthMate AI uses Artificial Intelligence to analyze user-provided wellness information and generate a personalized health report.

The AI provides:
- Wellness score
- Health summary
- Improvement suggestions
- Lifestyle tips
- Motivation message

### AI Instructions / System Prompt

The AI is instructed to act as a wellness assistant. It analyzes user data such as:

- Age
- Gender
- Water intake
- Sleep hours
- Exercise
- Diet quality
- Mood

and creates a helpful personalized wellness report.
